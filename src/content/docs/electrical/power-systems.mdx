## 📝 Companion Notes & Formulas

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="⚡ Balanced Three-Phase Systems">
    ### Three-Phase Power Relationships

    In a balanced three-phase system with line voltage $V_L$ and line current $I_L$:

    **Wye ($\text{Y}$) Connection:**
    $$
    V_L = \sqrt{3} V_{ph} \angle 30^\circ, \quad I_L = I_{ph}
    $$

    **Delta ($\Delta$) Connection:**
    $$
    V_L = V_{ph}, \quad I_L = \sqrt{3} I_{ph} \angle -30^\circ
    $$

    **Total Three-Phase Complex Power:**
    $$
    S_{3\phi} = \sqrt{3} V_L I_L^* = 3 V_{ph} I_{ph}^* = P_{3\phi} + jQ_{3\phi}
    $$
    $$
    P_{3\phi} = \sqrt{3} V_L I_L \cos(\theta) \quad [\text{W}]
    $$
  </TabItem>

  <TabItem label="🌐 Transmission Line Modeling">
    ### Medium-Length Line ($\Pi$-Model)

    The ABCD transmission matrix relates sending-end and receiving-end parameters:
    $$
    \begin{bmatrix}
      \mathbf{V}_S \\
      \mathbf{I}_S
    \end{bmatrix}
    =
    \begin{bmatrix}
      A & B \\
      C & D
    \end{bmatrix}
    \begin{bmatrix}
      \mathbf{V}_R \\
      \mathbf{I}_R
    \end{bmatrix}
    $$
    Where $AD - BC = 1$ for a reciprocal network.
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>
