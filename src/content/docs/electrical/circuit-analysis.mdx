## 📝 Study Notes & Formulas

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="⚡ Fundamental Circuit Laws">
    ### Kirchhoff's Laws & Ohm's Law

    **Ohm's Law:**
    $$
    V = I \cdot R \quad \Longleftrightarrow \quad I = \frac{V}{R}
    $$

    **Kirchhoff's Current Law (KCL):**
    $$
    \sum_{k=1}^{n} I_k = 0 \quad \text{(Algebraic sum of currents entering a node is zero)}
    $$

    **Kirchhoff's Voltage Law (KVL):**
    $$
    \sum_{k=1}^{m} V_k = 0 \quad \text{(Algebraic sum of voltages around a closed loop is zero)}
    $$

    ---

    ### Thévenin & Norton Equivalent Circuits
    Any linear, bilateral two-terminal network can be replaced by:
    - **Thévenin Equivalent**: An ideal voltage source $V_{th}$ in series with resistance $R_{th}$.
    - **Norton Equivalent**: An ideal current source $I_n$ in parallel with resistance $R_n$.
    
    $$
    I_n = \frac{V_{th}}{R_{th}}, \quad R_n = R_{th}
    $$
  </TabItem>

  <TabItem label="🌊 AC Steady-State & Phasors">
    ### Phasors & Impedance

    For sinusoidal excitations $v(t) = V_m \cos(\omega t + \phi)$, the phasor representation is $\mathbf{V} = V_{rms} \angle \phi$.

    **Impedance Elements:**
    - Resistor: $Z_R = R$
    - Inductor: $Z_L = j\omega L = \omega L \angle 90^\circ$
    - Capacitor: $Z_C = \frac{1}{j\omega C} = -\frac{j}{\omega C} = \frac{1}{\omega C} \angle -90^\circ$

    **Total Complex Power:**
    $$
    S = P + jQ = \mathbf{V}_{rms} \cdot \mathbf{I}_{rms}^*
    $$
    - Real Power: $P = |\mathbf{V}||\mathbf{I}|\cos(\theta)$ (Watts)
    - Reactive Power: $Q = |\mathbf{V}||\mathbf{I}|\sin(\theta)$ (VAR)
    - Apparent Power: $|S| = \sqrt{P^2 + Q^2}$ (VA)
  </TabItem>
