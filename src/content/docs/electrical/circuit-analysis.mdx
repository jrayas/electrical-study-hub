## 📝 Study Notes & Formulas

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="⚡ Fundamental Circuit Laws">
    ### Kirchhoff's Laws & Ohm's Law

    **Ohm's Law:**
    ```text
    V = I · R   <=>   I = V / R   <=>   R = V / I
    ```

    **Kirchhoff's Current Law (KCL):**
    ```text
    Σ I_k = 0   (The algebraic sum of currents entering any node is zero)
    ```

    **Kirchhoff's Voltage Law (KVL):**
    ```text
    Σ V_k = 0   (The algebraic sum of voltages around any closed loop is zero)
    ```

    ---

    ### Thévenin & Norton Equivalent Circuits
    Any linear, bilateral two-terminal network can be replaced by:
    - **Thévenin Equivalent**: An ideal voltage source `V_th` in series with resistance `R_th`.
    - **Norton Equivalent**: An ideal current source `I_n` in parallel with resistance `R_n`.
    
    ```text
    I_n = V_th / R_th,     R_n = R_th
    ```
  </TabItem>

  <TabItem label="🌊 AC Steady-State & Phasors">
    ### Phasors & Impedance

    For sinusoidal excitations `v(t) = V_m · cos(ωt + φ)`, the phasor representation is `V = V_rms ∠ φ`.

    **Impedance Elements:**
    - Resistor: `Z_R = R`
    - Inductor: `Z_L = jωL = ωL ∠ 90°`
    - Capacitor: `Z_C = 1 / (jωC) = -j / (ωC) = (1 / ωC) ∠ -90°`

    **Total Complex Power:**
    ```text
    S = P + jQ = V_rms · I_rms*
    ```
    - Real Power: `P = |V||I| cos(θ)` (Watts)
    - Reactive Power: `Q = |V||I| sin(θ)` (VAR)
    - Apparent Power: `|S| = sqrt(P² + Q²)` (VA)
  </TabItem>
